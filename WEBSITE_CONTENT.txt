  POSITIONING
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Entraguard is positioned as "A Parent-Centered School Companion for
  Real-Time Visibility, Safety, and Student Progress."

  It is a PARENT-FIRST mobile app. Parents get real-time classroom
  attendance notifications, grade and performance visibility, evaluation
  insights, and direct communication with their child's teachers — all in
  one secure app. Teachers mark attendance per subject and share grades;
  the school adopts the platform so records are official and verified.

  Business model: B2C2B — the PARENT pays, and the SCHOOL adopts. Messaging
  prioritizes emotion, trust, and simplicity over technical depth.
